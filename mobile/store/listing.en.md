Your best friends make the best rivals.

Throw pies, fly a mischievous pigeon, escape a trapped elevator or steal snacks with your team. Practice, invite a friend to a live duel, share a score challenge in supported games, or play a tournament.

Snack Heist also offers private 2v2 rooms for four human players. Share an invitation, find your friends and play for the rematch.

Internet connection required. Asynchronous score challenges are available for Chef Couic and Tour de Trop; other games offer live invitations. This version has no ads or in-app purchases. Game credits are free, fictional and cannot be exchanged for money or prizes.
