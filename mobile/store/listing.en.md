# English store copy — draft

Name: SlapTax — Friend Challenges
Apple subtitle: Snack heists and rematches
Google short description: Steal snacks, challenge friends and settle the score in cartoon mini-games.
Studio: Gignoski Games
Support: gignoski@gmail.com

Your best friends make the best rivals.

The Steward taxed snack time. At the Canteen, move with one stick, slap the carrier and bring home three crates before your rival. One button to slap or throw: choose your route and your moment.

Continue the heist across the Factory: divide the loot, stack the crates, return an explosive parcel or deliver a pie to a friend’s face. Practice, invite a friend to a live duel, share a supported score challenge or organize a tournament.

Snack Heist also offers private 2v2 rooms for four human players. Share an invitation, find your friends and play for the rematch.

Internet connection required. Asynchronous score challenges are available for Chef Couic and Tour de Trop; other games offer live invitations. This version has no ads or in-app purchases. Game credits are free, fictional and cannot be exchanged for money or prizes.

Questions or reports: gignoski@gmail.com
